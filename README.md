# Uber Leaps

Uber Leaps: a game made in Godot, where you have to deliver food... on a **bouncy ball**!

## How to Play
- WASD or Arrow Keys to move
- Grab orders from the superstore
- Deliver them to their proper customers
- Dodge the hedgehogs, cacti, and urchins!
- Pick up tape to fix your ball!

And remember... **Don't get POPPED**!
